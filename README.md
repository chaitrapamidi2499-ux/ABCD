# ABCD – Any Body Can Draw

**ABCD** is a beginner-friendly outdoor art circle for adults (21+), created to bring people together through creativity, self-expression, and meaningful connections.

A little space to pause, create, explore, and connect.

## About ABCD

ABCD is built around the idea that art isn't about perfection or artistic skills. It's about enjoying the process, experimenting with creativity, and giving yourself the time and space to explore something new.

Whether you're an experienced artist or someone picking up a paintbrush for the first time, there's a place for you here.

## What We Do

- Beginner-friendly art sessions
- Outdoor creative gatherings
- Guided art activities
- Opportunities to meet new people
- A welcoming space for creativity and self-expression

## About the Host

**Crafterina**

ABCD is an initiative by Crafterina, a creative venture founded by Chaitra, built around handmade art, creative experiences, and bringing people closer through art.

Through ABCD, Crafterina aims to create spaces where people can slow down, explore their creativity, and connect beyond their everyday routines.
