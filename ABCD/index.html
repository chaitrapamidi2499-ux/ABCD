So, What's ABCD?

ABCD stands for Any Body Can Draw. It's a recreational art gathering for anyone who wants to make things, slow down and enjoy the process. You don't need experience, a plan or a finished piece. You just need to show up.

ABCD is a recreational art gathering. It is not art therapy, and it is not a therapeutic or counselling service.

A Little Bit About Me

Hi, I'm Chaitra! A creative soul, an art enthusiast and someone who believes that creativity should be a part of everyday life.

I've always loved exploring different art forms, creating things by hand and bringing people together through creative experiences. I started Crafterina in 2018 as a way to share that love for art with others.

Over time, I realised that art doesn't always have to be about creating something beautiful or perfect. Sometimes, it's simply about slowing down, expressing yourself, trying something new and enjoying the process.

That's what inspired me to start ABCD, a little art circle where you don't have to be an artist to enjoy being creative.

I'm here to create a space where we can make a little art, meet new people and spend some time away from our everyday routines.

I can't wait to create with you! 🎨

Part 1 · Free Draw

Draw, doodle, colour or illustrate whatever you like. Use plain paper or the printed colouring sheets available on site. There's no prompt and no right way to do it.

Part 2 · Guided Art Reflection

Everyone gets the same creative reflection prompt, inspired by art therapy prompts. You can reflect quietly on paper and share your experience only if you want to. This is a creative exercise, not professional art therapy.

Questions, Answered

Do I need prior drawing experience?

No. Whether you've never held a pencil or you draw every day, you belong here.

Do I need to bring art supplies?

No. All art materials are provided. You can bring your own water bottle.

Is the session suitable for beginners?

Yes. ABCD is built for beginners as much as for experienced artists. There's no good or bad art here.

Can I attend alone?

Yes. You don't need to come with anyone. Just come as you are.

Is the sharing session compulsory?

No. Sharing is always optional. You can simply enjoy creating quietly.

Where will the session take place?

The venue is announced every Thursday for that Sunday's session. Exact location details are sent after you register, not published here in advance.

What happens if it rains?

If weather conditions make it unsafe or unsuitable to hold an outdoor session, ABCD may cancel it, and registered participants receive a full refund.

How do I confirm my registration?

Pay ₹100, fill in the registration form and upload your payment screenshot. Your registration is confirmed once your payment is verified.

Our Gentle Ground Rules

  1. There is no such thing as good or bad art. Every creation is valid. We're here to enjoy the process, not prove our skills.
  2. No experience required. Whether you've never held a paintbrush or have been creating for years, you belong here.
  3. No comparison. Don't compare your artwork with someone else's. Everyone has their own creative style and pace.
  4. No unsolicited criticism. Don't judge, correct or offer suggestions about someone's artwork unless they ask for feedback.
  5. Respect personal space. Don't touch, move or use another person's artwork or materials without permission.
  6. Sharing is always optional. You can talk about your artwork or simply enjoy creating quietly. Nobody is expected to explain what they make.
  7. Respect everyone's perspectives. Different ideas, experiences and forms of expression are welcome. No discrimination, bullying or insensitive remarks.
  8. Listen without interpreting. Don't assume what someone's artwork means or make personal judgments based on what they draw.
  9. Be present and participate at your own pace. There's no pressure to finish an artwork or follow someone else's approach.
  10. Respect confidentiality. Personal experiences shared during our gatherings stay within the group. Don't share someone's stories or photographs without consent.
  11. Photography requires consent. Always ask before photographing or recording someone or their artwork.
  12. Leave the space as you found it. Respect the park, dispose of waste responsibly and help keep our shared space clean.

Cancellation Policy

Please read these terms before registering. By completing your registration, you agree to them.

1. Participant Cancellation

  • Cancellations made at least 24 hours before the scheduled session are eligible for a full refund.
  • For cancellations made less than 24 hours before the session, you may request to reschedule to the next available session. Rescheduling is subject to availability in the next session and is not guaranteed.
  • No-shows are not eligible for a refund.

2. Cancellation by ABCD

If ABCD cancels a session for any reason, registered participants receive a full refund.

3. Weather-Related Cancellations

If weather conditions make it unsafe or unsuitable to hold an outdoor session, ABCD may cancel it, and participants receive a full refund.

4. Refund Processing

  • Approved refunds are processed within 2–3 working days.
  • Refunds are issued through the original payment method or via UPI, as applicable.

5. Registration Policy

  • Registrations are individual and valid only for the selected session.
  • Bookings cannot be transferred to another person or converted into credits for future sessions.
  • A rescheduled booking cannot be transferred to another person.

For cancellation or refund queries, contact the ABCD team through the details in the Contact section below.
